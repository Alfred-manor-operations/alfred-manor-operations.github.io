Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Who this covers

Alfred is a private assistant used by a single owner. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect data from visitors to this website.

What data Alfred accesses

With the owner’s explicit authorization, Alfred accesses the owner’s Google Calendar and Gmail data through Google’s official APIs. It accesses only the data needed for the task it has been asked to do, such as reading a schedule, creating or removing an event, or reading and drafting email.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests.
  • Access credentials are stored locally on the owner’s computer and are never published or shared.
  • To carry out a request, relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider that powers the assistant.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services

Alfred’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the connected account’s Google third-party access settings.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the Alfred-manor-operations GitHub organization.
